## The Fourteen Day Sprint

Security+ is a full domain sweep in two weeks if you treat it like a job, not a hobby. Budget 3 to 4 hours a day and don't skip days, momentum is most of what makes this timeline work.

---

## Phase 1: Domain Blueprint (Days 1-5)

Get the whole exam blueprint into your head through video before touching a practice question.

### Days 1-5

- **Task:** Watch Professor Messer's full Security+ SY0-701 course on YouTube, start to finish, at 1.25x to 1.5x speed
- **Focus:** Every domain, in order, no skipping around
- **Goal:** You're not trying to master anything yet, just building a map so nothing on the exam is a total stranger. Pause to write down anything that feels new, keep moving past anything that already feels familiar from day to day IT or homelab work.

---

## Phase 2: Target Weak Domains (Days 6-9)

Stop guessing where you're weak, let a real exam tell you.

### Day 6: Diagnostic

- Take one full-length practice exam cold, no review beforehand
- Score every domain separately, not just the overall percentage
- This is a baseline, not a pass/fail moment

### Days 7-9: Weak Domain Repair

- Re-watch only the Messer sections tied to your lowest two or three domains
- For anything that still doesn't click on a second watch, pull the matching chapter out of a study guide and read it once
- Skip domains you already scored well on, that time is better spent elsewhere

---

## Phase 3: Practice Exam Grind (Days 10-13)

This is where the score actually moves. One exam in the morning, full autopsy in the afternoon.

### Daily Routine, Days 10-13

- **Morning:** One new practice exam, timed, no notes, treat it like the real thing
- **Afternoon:** Go through every wrong answer, not just the ones you got wrong for a dumb reason. Write down why the correct answer is correct and why your answer was wrong, not just which letter to pick next time

---

## Day 14: Test Day

- Light review only: port numbers, protocol acronyms, anything you keep second-guessing
- No new material, no new practice exams
- Take the exam

---

## Resources

- **Video:** Professor Messer's Security+ SY0-701 course on YouTube. Free, modular, mapped directly to the current exam objectives. Good enough on its own for Phase 1.
- **Video, paid alternative:** Jason Dion's Udemy course if you want built-in quizzes and a more classroom feel.
- **Practice exams:** Dion Training or Professor Messer's practice tests. Closer to the real exam's phrasing than any free question bank.
- **Exam objectives:** Pull the official CompTIA SY0-701 objectives PDF and treat it as a checklist. If a term isn't on it, don't spend time on it.

---

## Test Day Tactics

- **Ports and acronyms are free points.** Flashcard the common ports (HTTPS 443, SSH 22, SFTP 22, and the rest) and the crypto acronyms (AES, RSA, SHA) early, they show up constantly and cost nothing to memorize.
- **PBQs go last.** The exam opens with a handful of performance-based questions, hands-on sims that eat time and cause panic if you let them. Flag and skip, clear every multiple choice question first, come back to the PBQs with whatever time is left.
- **Don't book the exam early.** Wait until you're consistently hitting 80-85% or better on fresh, first-attempt practice exams before you schedule a date.
